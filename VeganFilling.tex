This is a general-purpose vegan filling that I developed when I organized a pastel-making party and wanted to accommodate vegan guests.
\begin{description}

\item[Ingredients:]\ \\
	\begin{itemize}
	\item 1 large or two small russet potato (about 1 pound)
	\item 1 large carrot
	\item 3 ribs of cellery
	\item 1 medium onion
	\item 1/3 of a cup of diced sun-dried tomatoes
	\item 3 cloves of garlic
	\item 1/2 teaspoon of red pepper flakes
	\item 3 tablespoons of neutral cooking oil
	\item salt to taste (about 1 teaspoon)
	\item 1/2 teaspoon of sugar
	\item 3 Tablespoon of chopped parsley
	\end{itemize}

\item[Procedure:]\ \\
	\begin{enumerate}
	\item[Prep the vegetables]
	\begin{itemize}
        \item Boil or steam the potatoes in their jackets until they can be easily pierced with a sharp knife. Let them cool off.
        \item Peel the carrot and dice into very small dice.
        \item Put the diced carrot into a bowl and sprinkle with a small amount of salt and with the sugar.
        \item Dice the celery into very small dice.
        \item Dice the onion into very small dice (keep separate from the celery).
        \item Peel the garlic but do not dice it yet.
        \item Heat up the oil and add the diced carrots, including the liquid that the carrots have released. 
        \item Sautee the carrots, stirring until they are soft.
        \item Place a colander over a bowl and put the carrots including the oil on the colander to drain the oil.
        \item Return the oil to the pan.
        \item Add the diced onions to the pan and sautee, stirring, until the onions are translucent and at most very lightly golden.
        \item Add tree Tablespoons of water to the onions, cover the pan and cook for a few minutes to steam the onions. 
        \item Remove the lead and continue cooking until all the water has evaporated.
        \item Add the diced celery and the red pepper flakes and continue sauteeing.
        \item Peel and dice the cooked potatoes into small dice.
        \item Chop the garlic finely
        \item Create an empty spot on the center of the pan and let it get quite hot - it takes about a minute.
        \item Add the chopped garlic to the hot spot and cook, stirring on that spot only, for about 30 seconds.
        \item Add about 3 Tablespoons of water and stir the whole pot.
        \item Add the diced potatoes, the carrot, the chopped sun-dried tomatoes, and the salt.
        \item Cook stirring for a few minutes. 
        \item Taste for salt and adjust.
        \item Remove from the fire, add the chopped parsley and stir.
        \item Let it cool completely before using.
        \end{itemize}
  	\end{enumerate}
\end{description}
